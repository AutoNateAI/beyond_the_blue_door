## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a narrative of profound transformation: crossing the threshold from systemic friction into generational prosperity. The visual language marries the authoritative gravitas of institutional philanthropy with the deeply felt emotion of human ascension and breakthrough. 

The aesthetic is an editorial-grade fusion of **Architectural Minimalism** and **Luminous Glassmorphism**. Layouts balance rigorous, structured grids against atmospheric depth—employing deep nocturnal blues that transition into radiant, sunrise-inspired ambers and golds. Glassmorphic overlays with delicate hairline borders suggest clear portals and open doorways, grounding complex systemic diagrams (such as recursive human development cycles) in clarity, calm, and elevated distinction.

Every surface is engineered to inspire trust among high-conviction funders and institutional partners while delivering a warm, dignifying, and frictionless digital home for participants and community leaders.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around dynamic thresholds: night into dawn, obscurity into clarity.

- **Primary (`#0D1B2A` - Deep Midnight Navy):** Serves as the bedrock tone. Used for deep-canvas anchors, structural boundaries, header navigation, and high-impact editorial moments. Expresses enduring stability and institutional authority.
- **Secondary (`#1D4ED8` - Lapis Cobalt):** The active catalyst. Applied to interactive flows, progress state indicators, primary calls to action, focus rings, and active graph nodes. Represents systemic momentum and forward journey.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` / `#F59E0B` - Radiant Amber Gold):** The beacon. Reserved for moments of ascension, achievements, conversion triggers, milestone badges, and the luminous edge lighting of glassmorphic containers. Accompanying tint (`#FEF3C7`) provides soft celebratory fills.
- **Neutral (`#475569` - Slate):** Delivers balanced readability across micro-copy and secondary text, paired against `#0F172A` for primary body copy.
- **Canvas & Surface System:** 
  - Ground layer: Warm Pearl Porcelain (`#F8FAFC`).
  - Elevated card surfaces: Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) with deliberate light-transmitting opacities (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`).
  - High-contrast modal layers: Deep Void Navy (`#0A1128`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy creates tension and dialogue between human depth and technological precision.

- **Editorial Headings (Playfair Display):** Bestowed with classic proportions and expressive serifs, these capture the aspirational, narrative dimension of the institution. Used for manifesto statements, section anchors, and transformative chapter titles.
- **System Interface & Data (Plus Jakarta Sans):** A modern, geometric sans-serif engineered for digital environments. Handles dense informational architecture, conversion pathways, tabular figures, process step titles, and interactive graph nodes with pristine legibility.
- **Labeling & Micro-Typography:** High-order labels, tracking tags, and meta-specifiers always use `label-caps` set in uppercase with deliberate letter-spacing (`0.08em`), establishing clean spatial orientation across complex visual dashboards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column responsive fluid grid pinned within an expansive maximum container width of 1280px.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and 48px (`3rem`) page margins. Asymmetrical columns (e.g., 5-col narrative manifesto paired with 7-col interactive recursive diagrams) are favored over basic equal divisions.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px margins. Multistep pipeline flows wrap into modular 2x2 grids.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 20px (`1.25rem`) margins. Complex horizontal flows convert strictly to vertical timeline feeds.

Vertical rhythm adheres to an 8px modular baseline scale. Generous section breathing room (`space-2xl`) prevents cognitive fatigue during data exploration, allowing each institutional metric and human story to command presence.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system conveys optical clarity rather than simple physical weight, heavily relying on **Ambient Luminous Shadows** and **Selective Translucency**.

- **Level 1 (Subtle Inset / Grounded Rail):** Outlined with `1px solid rgba(13, 27, 42, 0.06)`, zero drop shadow. Used for track rails in pipeline ladders, graph connection paths, and unselected filter chips.
- **Level 2 (Glassmorphic Metric Surface):** Fill: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`, backdrop-filter: `blur(12px)`. Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.70)` with an upper interior highlight of `rgba(254, 243, 199, 0.40)`. Drop shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(13, 27, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Ascension / Active Focus):** Drop shadow: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(13, 27, 42, 0.04)`. Border: `1px solid rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.25)`.
- **Level 4 (Capital / Partner Modal Overlays):** Deep navy context (`#0A1128`) layered over a `rgba(10, 17, 40, 0.65)` backdrop with `blur(16px)`. Border: `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.20)`. Ambient golden bottom glow: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture balances clean structural stability (`0.5rem` / `8px` baseline radius) with gentle ergonomic softness for interactive touchpoints.

- **Standard Containers & Cards:** Set at `rounded-md` (`0.5rem`), presenting crisp, precise corners that communicate institutional discipline and architectural rigor.
- **Large Surfaces & Modals:** Scaled to `rounded-lg` (`1rem`) and `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem`), softening large surfaces and framing recursive system loops cleanly.
- **Interactive Controls, Badges, & Chips:** Use fully rounded pill geometries (`9999px`) to maintain tactile friendliness, encourage interaction, and offset the structured typography.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Sovereign CTA:** Deep Navy (`#0D1B2A`) background, crisp white typography, pill-shaped (`9999px`). Features a 1px inner border in gold (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)`). On hover, subtle translateY(-1px) with an ambient gold-and-cobalt shadow bloom.
- **Ascension CTA (High-Intent Conversion):** Gradient background from Rich Lapis (`#1D4ED8`) to Radiant Amber (`#D97706`), bold sans-serif type, elevated by a directional drop shadow. Reserved for high-value partner pledges and enrollment.
- **Ghost / Outlined:** Transparent surface with a 1px border of `#0D1B2A` at 20% opacity. Shifts to solid navy on active hover.

### Journey Ladders & Pipeline Flows
- Continuous vertical or horizontal track rails in slate at 12% opacity.
- Completed milestones display a solid Cobalt (`#1D4ED8`) circular node enclosing a gold checkmark glyph.
- The active, in-progress stage glows with a concentric amber halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 6px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.16)`).

### Recursive Vertical Graph Nodes (Self → Child → Family → Community → Self)
- Vertical node series enclosed in light glassmorphic shells.
- Each node level features a distinct icon badge: 
  - *Self:* Single luminous point.
  - *Child:* Dual ascending rings.
  - *Family:* Shield motif.
  - *Community:* Convergent constellation.
- An animated, directional gradient stroke connects each node, looping back from Community to Self to visually communicate generational compounding.

### Metric Cards
- Glassmorphic translucent white background over porcelain backdrops.
- Top label: `label-caps` in Slate (`#475569`).
- Primary KPI: `label-metric` in Deep Navy (`#0D1B2A`).
- Delta / Proof badge: Pill-shaped micro-badge in soft gold (`#FEF3C7`) with amber text (`#D97706`) detailing generational impact percentage.

### Form Inputs & Conversion Touchpoints
- Background: `#FFFFFF` with a subtle slate border (`#CBD5E1`).
- Focus state: Border transitions to Cobalt (`#1D4ED8`) with an atmospheric focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.15)`).
- Input labels leverage `title-md` for legibility, with contextual helper text positioned below in `body-sm`.
